The Forgetting Curve
Explained Simply

Why you forget 70% in 24 hours — and the 10-minute trick to keep 90%.

You study today. Tomorrow you remember 30%. After 1 week, you remember 10%.

This is not because you are lazy. This is called the Forgetting Curve. A German scientist named Ebbinghaus discovered it 100 years ago. Your brain is the same.

Your brain forgets on purpose.
To save energy. You must tell it: "This is important."

What Happens In Your Brain?

When you learn something new, your brain makes a weak connection. If you don't use it, brain thinks: "Not important, delete it."

But if you recall it again after 1 day, brain thinks: "Oh, we needed this again? Let's make it stronger."

Each recall makes the connection stronger and forgetting slower.

The 10-Minute Trick

After you study, do this:

1. After 10 minutes: Close book, recall for 2 min.
2. After 1 day: Recall for 5 min.
3. After 3 days: Recall for 5 min.
4. After 7 days: Recall for 5 min.

Total extra time: 17 minutes. Result: You remember 90% instead of 10%.

Why Re-Reading Doesn't Fix It

Re-reading makes you feel familiar. "Oh yes I saw this." But in exam, you need to RECALL, not recognize.

Familiarity is a trick. Recall is the real test.

Real Example - Medical Student

Mona studies Pneumonia:

  • Monday: Reads Pneumonia for 1 hour. Remembers 100%.
  • Tuesday: Does NOT review. Remembers 30%.
  • Next Monday: Remembers 10%. She must study again from zero.

Ahmed studies Pneumonia:

  • Monday: Reads 1 hour.
  • Tuesday: Recalls 10 min. Back to 100%.
  • Friday: Recalls 10 min. Back to 100%.
  • Next Monday: Still remembers 90%.

Same 1 hour of reading. Ahmed added 20 minutes of recall. Ahmed wins.

Your Action Today - 10 Minutes

Pick something you studied yesterday. Close book. Write what you remember. Check gaps. That's it. You just beat the Forgetting Curve.
