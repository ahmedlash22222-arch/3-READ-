Active Recall:
The #1 Study Method

Reading feels productive. Testing yourself IS productive. This is the difference between passing and failing.

Two students. Same 3 hours.

Student A: Reads Davidson chapter 3 times. Highlights everything. Feels confident.
Student B: Reads 1 time, then closes book and tries to write everything she remembers. Struggles. Feels stupid.

In the exam, Student B wins. Every time.

If you can recall it with the book closed,
you can recall it in the exam.

What Is Active Recall?

Active Recall = Close the book. Try to remember. Check what you missed.

Passive = Re-read, re-watch video, re-highlight. Feels easy, but brain does nothing.

Your brain learns by pulling out information, not by putting it in.

How To Do It - 3 Easy Ways

1. The Blank Paper Method (Best for Davidson)

  • Read one disease for 15 min.
  • Close book.
  • Take blank paper. Write: Definition, Cause, Symptoms, Investigations, Treatment.
  • Open book. Check what you missed in red pen.
  • Recall again tomorrow.
