How To Study When You Have
No Time / No Motivation

Motivation comes AFTER you start. Not before.

You don't need motivation. You need a start so small you can't say no.

Don't wait to feel ready.
Start small, motivation will follow.

The 5-Minute Rule

Tell yourself: "I will study for just 5 minutes."

Set a timer. Open book. Do 5 minutes.

After 5 minutes, you can stop. 90% of the time, you won't stop. Starting was the hard part.

The 15-Minute Minimum Day

On your worst day, your minimum is 15 minutes. Not 0.

15 minutes of Anki is better than 0. 15 minutes keeps the habit alive. 0 breaks it.

Rule: Never miss twice. Bad day? 15 min. Next day? Back to normal.

Phone In Another Room

Willpower doesn't work. Environment works.

  • Phone in another room, on silent
  • Open only 1 tab: Anki or your book
  • Water + paper on table
  • Tell yourself: "25 min, then I check phone"

You don't have a focus problem. You have a phone problem.

No Time? Use Dead Time

No 2-hour block? Use:

  • Bus / waiting: 10 Anki cards
  • Before sleep: Recall 3 things you learned today, no book
  • Morning: 5 min recall of yesterday before phone

If You Feel Guilty, Read This

Everyone feels behind. Everyone feels lazy sometimes. Even toppers.

Difference is not feeling. Difference is: they start for 5 minutes anyway.

You don't need to study 8 hours today. You need to start for 5 minutes today.
