Anki: How To Use It
Without Drowning

Anki is powerful. But 90% of students use it wrong and end up with 500 cards due. Here is the simple system.

Anki is not magic. Anki is just Spaced Repetition + Active Recall in an app.

It shows you a card right before you forget it. You try to recall. You press Again / Good / Easy. It schedules it again.

20 good cards a day beats
200 bad cards you never finish.

The Golden Rules

Rule 1: Max 20 New Cards A Day

If you add 100 cards today, in 7 days you will have 700 cards due. You will quit. Set new cards to 20 per day. That's it.

Rule 2: 1 Fact Per Card

BAD card: "Tell me everything about Heart Failure"
GOOD card: Front: "What is first-line drug for Heart Failure?" Back: "ACE inhibitor - Enalapril"

Short cards = fast reviews = you actually finish.

Rule 3: Never Skip Reviews

New cards you can pause. Reviews you must do every day. Even if you are tired, do reviews. 15 min is enough.

What Buttons To Press?

  • Again (<10 min): You had no idea. Press this honestly.
  • Good: You remembered with effort. This is most cards.
  • Easy: You knew it instantly. Press only if too easy.

Don't press Good when you actually forgot. You are lying to your future self.

What Decks To Use?

  • Make your own: Best for Davidson. After you read a disease, make 10 cards from it. You remember because you made them.
  • Use pre-made: Good for Anatomy and Pharmacology. Search "AnKing" or "Zanki" - but don't download 20,000 cards. Pick one deck for your year.

My Daily Anki Routine - 30 Min

Morning 15 min: Do all Reviews.
Evening 15 min: Read 1 Davidson topic, make 10 new cards, do them.
That's it. No more, no less.

Your Action Today

1. Download Anki (free on computer, AnkiDroid free on phone).
2. Make 1 deck: "Davidson - My Cards"
3. Make 10 cards from yesterday's topic.
4. Do them tomorrow. Done.

When To Delete Cards?

If a card is confusing, too long, or you always press Again - delete it and remake it shorter. Bad cards waste your time.
