NotebookLM:
Your AI Study Partner

Upload Davidson, get quizzes, podcasts, tables. Free. This changes everything for medical students.

Imagine this: You upload Davidson Chapter on Heart Failure. AI reads it. Then it makes a podcast for you, makes a quiz, makes a table, and answers your questions - only from that chapter.

That's NotebookLM. Made by Google. Free.

Don't ask ChatGPT to guess.
Ask NotebookLM to read YOUR book.

What Is NotebookLM?

NotebookLM is a free AI notebook. You give it sources - PDF, slides, YouTube video. It only answers from those sources. No hallucinations, no guessing.

ChatGPT guesses from internet. NotebookLM reads YOUR Davidson and answers from Davidson.

How To Start - 5 Minutes

1. Go to notebooklm.google.com
2. Sign in with Google account
3. Click "New Notebook"
4. Click "Add Source" > Upload your Davidson PDF chapter
5. Wait 1 minute. Done!

5 Ways To Use It For Medicine

1. Audio Overview - Podcast

Click "Audio Overview". It makes a 10-min podcast like 2 people talking about your chapter. Listen in bus, while walking, while cooking. Best for revision.

2. Quiz - Test Yourself

Ask: "Make 20 MCQs from this chapter, with answers at the end." Do them book closed. This is Active Recall, automatic.

3. Tables - Compare Diseases

Ask: "Make a table comparing Asthma vs COPD: cause, symptoms, treatment." Copy table to your notes. Perfect for exams.

4. Explain Simply

Ask: "Explain Heart Failure like to a 12-year-old patient." It will simplify Davidson for you. Feynman Technique, automatic.

5. Ask Questions

Ask: "What are the causes of Pneumonia in this chapter?" It will answer and show you exactly which page it came from.

Best Prompts - Copy These

For Quiz:
"Make 20 exam-style MCQs from this source, with answers explained."

For Table:
"Make a comparison table for all diseases in this chapter."

For Summary:
"Summarize this chapter in 1 page, only most important points for exam."

For OSCE:
"Make a 5-min OSCE script for this disease: history, examination, management."

Warning - Don't Cheat Yourself

Don't use NotebookLM to SKIP reading. Use it AFTER you read, to test and review. If you only listen to podcast and never recall, you will still forget.

Rule: Read first -> Recall -> Then use NotebookLM to check.

For Sudan Students - No Laptop?

NotebookLM works on phone browser too. Upload PDF from phone, listen to Audio Overview. You don't need a computer.
