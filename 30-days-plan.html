Your 30-Day 3 Read Plan

All 11 articles. One plan. 30 days to change how you study forever.

Don't try to do everything at once. Follow this plan.

30 days. 3 Reads a day.
That's all it takes.

Week 1 - Days 1-7: Build The Base

  • Day 1-2: Read Active Recall + Spaced Repetition. Start Anki - 20 cards a day.
  • Day 3-4: Read Forgetting Curve + How To Read Davidson. Do your first 3 Read on Davidson.
  • Day 5-7: Read Feynman + Flashcards. Teach 1 topic to a friend.

Daily habit: 1x 3 Read (25 min) + 1x Anki (15 min) + 1x Recall before sleep (5 min)

Week 2 - Days 8-14: Add Power Tools

  • Day 8-9: Read Interleaving + Consolidation. Mix topics, don't do 1 chapter for 3 hours.
  • Day 10-11: Read NotebookLM + Pharma. Make 1 family table for Pharma.
  • Day 12-14: Read Anatomy. Draw 1 system from memory.

Week 3 - Days 15-21: Exam Mode

  • Day 15-17: Read No Time / No Motivation. Set your 15-min minimum day.
  • Day 18-21: Do 2 past papers. Use Feynman to fix weak topics. Review Anki daily.

Rule: If you miss a day, never miss twice.

Week 4 - Days 22-30: Finish Strong

  • Day 22-25: Only recall. No new reading. Close book, recall, check.
  • Day 26-28: OSCE practice + teach friends.
  • Day 29-30: Light review only. Sleep 8 hours. You are ready.
