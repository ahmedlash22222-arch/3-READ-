How To Read Davidson /
Bailey Without Forgetting

Davidson is 1000 pages. You don't need to read all of it. You need to read it right.

Most students open Davidson, start from page 1, read for 2 hours, highlight everything, and remember nothing.

There is a better way. 40 minutes, 1 disease, and you remember it.

Don't read to finish.
Read to remember.

The 5-25-10 Method

5 MIN - PREVIEW
Don't read yet. Look at headings, boxes, tables, summary at end. Ask: "What is this chapter about? What are the 3 diseases here?"

25 MIN - READ
Read ONE disease only. Not whole chapter. Read with a pen. Underline only 1 line per paragraph. If you underline everything, you underlined nothing.

10 MIN - RECALL
Close book. Take 1 paper. Write: Definition, Cause, Symptoms, Investigations, Treatment. Check gaps. Done.

Total: 40 minutes for 1 disease. And you remember it.

What To Skip In Davidson?

  • READ: Boxes, tables, summaries, clinical features, treatment.
  • SKIP first time: Rare syndromes, long epidemiology numbers, history of disease.
  • COME BACK later: If you have time, read details. If not, you already know 80% that comes in exam.

The 1-Page Summary Method

After you read a disease, make 1 page summary - by hand, not copy-paste.

Top: Disease name + 1 line definition
Middle: Table: Cause | Symptoms | Signs
Bottom: Investigations + Treatment in 5 lines
Side: 1 comparison: "How is this different from similar disease?"

Before exam, you don't re-read Davidson. You read your 1-page summaries. 20 pages = whole course.

How To Use Davidson + NotebookLM Together

1. Read disease with 5-25-10.
2. Upload that chapter to NotebookLM.
3. Ask: "Make 10 MCQs from this disease."
4. Do MCQs book closed.
5. Make your 1-page summary.

That's a perfect study session.

What About Bailey For Surgery?

Same method. Bailey is even bigger. Pick ONE topic: e.g. Appendicitis. Preview 5, Read 25, Recall 10. Don't try to read whole Bailey in one week.

Common Mistake

Don't read Davidson AND lecture slides AND YouTube for same topic in one day. Pick ONE source first. Learn it. Then use second source only for gaps.
