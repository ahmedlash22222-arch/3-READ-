How To Remember Anatomy
Without Memorizing 1000 Muscles

Where? What does it do? What if it breaks? Draw it, touch it, link it to a patient.

Anatomy is not a list to memorize. It is a map to understand.

If you memorize names without function, you forget in 2 days.

Don't memorize anatomy.
Use anatomy.

The 3 Questions For Every Structure

1. Where is it? Point to it on your own body. Touch it.
2. What does it do? What movement? What does it supply?
3. What if it breaks? If this nerve is cut, what will patient lose?

Example: Median nerve - Where? In arm/hand. What? Flexes fingers. Break? Can't make OK sign, hand of benediction.

Now you will never forget median nerve.

Draw It - Bad Drawing Is Better

Don't look at perfect Netter pictures and think you learned it.

Take blank paper. Draw the brachial plexus in 2 minutes, from memory. Bad drawing, wrong lines — that's okay. Check, fix, redraw. After 3 times, you own it.

Link It To A Patient

Don't learn: "Femoral artery is in femoral triangle"
Learn: "Patient with stab wound in groin - femoral artery bleeding - where do I press?"

Clinical link = memory that stays.

How To Study Anatomy In 30 Minutes

  • 0-10 min: Look at diagram, ask 3 questions
  • 10-20 min: Draw it from memory on blank paper
  • 20-30 min: Teach it to a friend: "If this breaks, what happens?"

Common Mistake

Don't read anatomy for 3 hours straight. Do 1 region, then stop. Recall tomorrow. Spacing works for anatomy too.
